This is the MRV Nexora Solutions business website and supporting project platform. It brings the public-facing brand experience together with the tools used to manage packages, project enquiries and client communication.

PROJECT STATUS

Fully developed as our own website and platform. The public showcase and admin capabilities are maintained as the business evolves.

Key features & scope

  • Responsive business website with service and portfolio pages
  • Service packages and country-based pricing presentation
  • Project enquiry submission and confirmation email workflow
  • Admin dashboard for managing enquiries, packages and project information
  • Client input portal and project follow-up workflows
