FLEXIBLE SERVICE OPTIONS

Your website.
Your choice.

Choose how you want to manage your domain, hosting and ongoing website care. You keep ownership of your accounts.

OPTION 01

Development Only

We design, develop and launch your website using the domain and hosting you already own or arrange yourself.

  • Website development and agreed features
  • Connect your existing domain
  • Deployment support for your hosting
  • 30-day post-launch bug-fixing support
Discuss this option ↗
OPTION 02

Development + Domain Setup

Buy your domain directly from a registrar in an account in your name. We guide you through the purchase and connect it to your website.

  • Everything in Development Only
  • Domain selection and registration guidance
  • DNS configuration and domain connection
  • Renewal date and ownership handover guidance
Discuss this option ↗
OPTION 03

Complete Website Setup

A coordinated setup for your website, domain, hosting and professional email. Third-party accounts remain in your name.

  • Website development and launch
  • Domain and hosting setup assistance
  • Business email / mailbox integration
  • Payment gateway integration when required
  • 30-day post-launch bug-fixing support
Discuss this option ↗
You stay in control. Domain, hosting, email and payment-provider accounts are registered and owned by you. You pay providers directly for registration, subscriptions, renewals and transaction fees. MRV Nexora provides setup and technical assistance as agreed.

OPTIONAL, NEVER REQUIRED

Ongoing website care.

Every project includes 30 days of post-launch bug-fixing support. After that, choose a care plan only if you want ongoing support.
